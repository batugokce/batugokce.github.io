Privacy Policy for TapAssist Auto Clicker

Effective date: 2026-04-26

TapAssist Auto Clicker ("the App") respects your privacy.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to external servers.

App settings and automation preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Permissions Used

  • Accessibility Service: Used only to perform user-configured tap/swipe automation.
  • Draw over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used only to show floating overlay controls.

These permissions are required for core app functionality and are used only when enabled by the user.

3. Data Sharing

The App does not sell personal information.

The App does not share user data with third parties for advertising or profiling.

4. Data Retention

Data remains on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear app data.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to keep app data secure on-device.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates are reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact
