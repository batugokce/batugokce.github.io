Privacy Policy for FloatScreen

Effective date: 2026-07-31

FloatScreen ("the App") lets you show a text note, countdown timer, or selected image over other Android apps. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. The App does not include advertising, analytics, accounts, cloud sync, or tracking SDKs.

Text you enter, timer values, and selected images are processed only on your device to provide the overlay feature. FloatScreen does not upload selected images or their contents.

2. Permissions Used

  • Draw over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used only to show the text, timer, and image overlays that you explicitly start. You can revoke this permission at any time in Android system settings.
  • Notifications: Used for the foreground-service notification while an overlay is active, so Android can indicate that the overlay service is running.
  • Selected image access: When you select an image with Android's system file picker, the App receives access only to that selected image and uses it only to display the image overlay.

3. Data Sharing

The App does not sell personal information and does not share user data with third parties for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

FloatScreen does not maintain a server-side account or database. Overlay content is handled locally on your device. You can remove local app data through Android system settings or by uninstalling the App. Where supported by your device, you can also revoke access to a selected image through Android system settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children or any other users.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
